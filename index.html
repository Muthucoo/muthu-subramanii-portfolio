01 / Profile

Connecting the detail of operations with the larger picture.

My experience has moved across different operating environments, but a common thread has remained: helping people, information and workstreams come together reliably.

At Standard Chartered Global Business Services, I supported delivery across 10+ enterprise-wide global programmes, coordinated stakeholders across APAC, ASEAN, EMEA, the UK and the US, and maintained management information reporting, programme dashboards and governance records. I received a formal promotion to Associate Manager during this period.

My earlier experience at Ford involved international assignment and domestic relocation documentation, allowance and payslip checks, and PeopleSoft-supported HR operations.
